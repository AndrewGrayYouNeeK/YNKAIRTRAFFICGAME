YNK Air Traffic privacy policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

YNK Air Traffic is a game published by YouNeeK. This policy covers the iOS app, the Android app, and the website that ships the same game.

Data YouNeeK collects

YouNeeK does not collect, sell, or share personal data. There is no account, no login, and no analytics or advertising SDK. Scores, unlocked airports, and the tutorial choice stay on the device in local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech

The mic button is optional. The game works with the on-screen buttons and the typed clearance. When you tap the mic, the app uses the microphone and Apple's Speech framework (or Android speech recognition) to turn what you say into a clearance. YouNeeK does not receive or store the audio or the transcript.

On iPhone, recognition stays on the device when that iPhone supports on-device recognition for English. If it does not, iOS may send the audio to Apple to transcribe it. That transfer is between the device and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, and YouNeeK is not a party to it.

Network use

The game is bundled inside the app and plays offline. If the phone is online, a shift may request weather for the airport you picked from Open-Meteo. That request sends the airport's published latitude and longitude, not your location. If the request fails, the shift uses weather stored in the game. Map screens elsewhere in the website may load map tiles. Those screens are not required to play the tower game.

App Store privacy label

Answers for App Store Connect → App Privacy:

  • Data used to track you: No.
  • Data linked to you: None.
  • Data not linked to you: None.
  • Data collection: Data Not Collected.

Do not declare microphone audio as collected. YouNeeK does not receive it. The microphone and speech-recognition permission prompts are still required, and the usage text in the app explains them.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
